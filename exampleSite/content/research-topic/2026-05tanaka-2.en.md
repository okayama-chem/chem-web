### Harnessing Light for Chemical Transformations: What is Photoredox Catalysis?

Photoredox catalysis is a powerful method in chemistry that uses light energy to facilitate chemical reactions. Salt-based organophotoredox catalysts are particularly effective because of their strong oxidizing properties. They are excellent at oxidizing substances rich in electrons (electron-rich substrates) and can drive a diverse range of chemical transformations through a process called a reductive quenching cycle. Until now, most conventional salt-based organic photocatalysts have predominantly contained oxygen and nitrogen atoms.

### Unlocking New Possibilities with Sulfur: Development of Novel Catalysts

Our research group has recently made a breakthrough by developing three new types of sulfur-containing salt-based organophotoredox catalysts. This is a significant advancement because incorporating sulfur into the catalyst structure can lead to unique catalytic properties and enable new chemical reactions that were previously difficult. This discovery opens up a new frontier in photoredox chemistry.

### Applications in Diverse Chemical Reactions and Future Prospects

This review article highlights the latest advances in these novel sulfur-containing organophotoredox catalysts and their applications in photoredox catalysis. These sulfur-based catalysts are expected to exhibit strong oxidative properties, potentially making challenging chemical reactions more accessible. The development of such new catalysts holds promise for contributing to the synthesis of pharmaceuticals, functional materials, and other valuable compounds that enrich our lives.
